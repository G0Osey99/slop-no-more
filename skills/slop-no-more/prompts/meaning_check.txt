Report a difference only if it falls into one of these kinds:
- **Facts and numbers.** A number, date, quantity, statistic, or result is changed, dropped, or added.
- **Names and references.** A person, place, organization, title, citation, or source is changed, dropped, or added.
- **Hedges and modal strength.** A claim is now stronger or weaker than the original states it, for example:
  - may/might/could vs. does; suggests/indicates vs. shows/proves;
  - some vs. most vs. all; often vs. always;

- **Claims and positions.** A claim, position, condition, qualification, reason, goal, request, or question is dropped, or one is added that the original doesn't make. This includes a feeling or reaction the author reports.
- **Concrete details.** An image, example, object, action, setting, or item in a list of details is dropped or changed. An anecdote counts as changed if its frame changed: one occasion vs. a habit, who did what, when, or in what order.
- **Dialogue and quotation.** Quoted or reported words are changed or dropped.

Do NOT report:
- **Evaluative adjectives and intensifiers.** Examples: "invaluable", "incredibly", "truly", "really", "crucial", "pivotal", "profound", "deeply", "significant" used as praise. A different degree word on a claim that is still there doesn't count either. These are voice, not meaning: "her advice was invaluable" and "her advice helped me a lot" make the same claim.

- **Changes of register or word choice**, sentence order within a paragraph, or sentence boundaries.
- **Anything the rewrite says in different words.**
